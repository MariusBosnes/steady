Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026 · Contact: mariusolsen13@gmail.com

1. Who we are

Steady is a Google Sheets template with Apps Script automation for freelancers and small businesses (“the App”), provided by Marius Olsen (“we”, “us”).

2. What Steady is

Steady runs inside the user’s own Google account. After you make a copy of the template and authorize the script, the App processes data stored in that Google Sheet and related Google services you choose to use (for example Drive files and email).

3. Information the App processes

Depending on how you use Steady, the App may process:

  • Business and client details you enter in the spreadsheet (names, emails, addresses, amounts, notes).
  • Invoice, quote, project, payment, expense, and related document data.
  • Files generated by the App, such as PDF invoices or quotes saved to your Google Drive.
  • Email content you choose to send through the App (for example invoice emails or reminders).
  • Settings you configure (logo URL, numbering, tax settings, payment links, reminder options).

4. Google user data and OAuth scopes

When you authorize Steady, Google may grant the App access to the following scopes solely to provide App features in your account:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/spreadsheets — read and update your Steady spreadsheet.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive — create and manage PDF/document files generated by the App.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/script.send_mail — send emails as you when you request invoice delivery or reminders.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/script.container.ui — show menus, sidebars, and dialogs inside Sheets.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/script.scriptapp — install triggers used for automation.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/script.external_request — fetch optional external resources (for example a public logo image URL).

The App’s use of information received from Google APIs complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. How we use data

Steady uses the data listed above only to:

  • Operate CRM, quoting, invoicing, payment, expense, and reporting features.
  • Generate PDFs and store them where you instruct the App to store them.
  • Send emails or reminders you explicitly enable or trigger.
  • Maintain workbook structure, settings, health checks, and backups inside your account.

We do not sell personal data. We do not use Google user data for advertising. We do not use Google user data for independent AI/ML model training.

6. Data storage and access

For the standard “Make a copy” distribution model, your spreadsheet data remains in your Google account. The developer does not receive ongoing access to your copied workbook unless you separately share that file or contact support and voluntarily share information.

Support emails you send to mariusolsen13@gmail.com are stored in ordinary email systems for support purposes.

7. Sharing

The App does not share your Google user data with third parties for their own purposes. Google processes data according to Google’s own terms and privacy policy when you use Google Sheets, Drive, Gmail, and related services.

8. Retention

Data in your Google Sheet and Drive remains under your control until you delete it or revoke the App’s access. Support emails are retained only as long as needed to respond and keep a reasonable support record.

9. Your choices

  • You can stop using the App at any time by deleting your copy of the spreadsheet.
  • You can revoke Apps Script / OAuth access in your Google Account security settings.
  • You can disable reminder/automation features in Settings.
  • You can request support help by emailing us.

10. Children’s privacy

Steady is intended for business users and is not directed at children under 16.

11. Changes

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will change when we do. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.

12. Contact

Questions about privacy or Google data use: mariusolsen13@gmail.com
