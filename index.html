Why Steady asks for Google permissions

Steady is an Apps Script template bound to a Google Sheet. When you authorize it, Google may show a consent screen. The app only uses permissions needed to run inside your Google account:

  • Google Sheets — read and write your Steady workbook.
  • Google Drive — create invoice/quote PDF files you generate.
  • Gmail / send mail — email invoices and optional reminders as you.
  • Apps Script UI & triggers — menus, sidebars, shortcuts, and scheduled jobs.
  • External requests — fetch optional resources such as a logo image URL.

Your business data stays in your Google account. See the privacy policy for full details.
